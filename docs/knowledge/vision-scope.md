# Vision Scope

## Phase
Phase 1: High-Level Scoping (System Baseline)

## Problem Statement
- Define the core business problem this product solves.
- Clarify who is impacted and why it matters now.

## Product Vision
- Describe the intended future state and value proposition.
- State the desired customer and business outcomes.

## Target Users and Stakeholders
- Primary user personas
- Secondary users
- Business stakeholders

## In Scope (MVP)
- High-level capabilities included in Phase 1 product scope.

## Out of Scope
- Items explicitly excluded from the MVP.

## Assumptions
- Key assumptions used to define the scope and priorities.

## Constraints
- Time, budget, compliance, technology, and organizational constraints.

## Dependencies
- Internal and external dependencies affecting delivery.

## Risks and Mitigations
- Major risks to scope or execution and mitigation plans.

## Success Metrics
- Measurable outcomes that indicate the scope and vision are successful.

## Prioritization Approach
- Framework used (MoSCoW, WSJF, RICE, Kano) and rationale.

## Approval and Sign-off
- Owners, reviewers, and approval date.
